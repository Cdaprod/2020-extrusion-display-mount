# V005: Saddle-to-support gusset and tapered opening

Source: IMG_9096.jpeg, green rear lower triangle and two arrows.

## Rear saddle reinforcement
Each support arm gains a triangular YZ gusset: lower corner at the saddle rear wall, upper corner at the same wall, and upper-rear corner near the arm foot. The gusset extends from Z=-7.8 mm to approximately Z=15.75 mm. The front edge overlaps the saddle rear wall by 0.8 mm. It is one fused solid with the arm and saddle, not a separate print.

## Aperture
The opening is a four-sided tapered cut with analytic rounded corners: front Y=16 mm, rear Y=40 mm at Z=44 mm, rear Y=30 mm at Z=96 mm, with 5 mm corner radius. This produces a forward-shifted opening whose back edge follows the general direction of the exterior rear diagonal. Dimensions are configurable and provisional; the cut must remain clear of the external boundaries.
